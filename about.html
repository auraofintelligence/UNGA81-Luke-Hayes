Minjerribah / North Stradbroke Island, Queensland, Australia.

This independent personal contribution responds to the UNGA81 theme, “Restoring Trust, Managing Transformation: A United Nations That Delivers for All”. It connects the author’s work on intelligence, ownership, livelihoods, democratic participation and possible futures.

The proposals are invitations to inquiry and practical experimentation. Their inclusion does not imply that a programme is operating, a treaty interpretation is authoritative, or an institution endorses this contribution.

and sharing.

Each page has its own AI-generated concept illustration. These imagined places are visual interpretations, not photographs, project renders based on engineering, or evidence of completed work.

The site uses no analytics, advertising, account system or tracking cookies. Search happens in your browser. GitHub hosts the site and may process standard access logs; external links follow their own privacy terms.

The text works without JavaScript. Motion respects your device’s reduced-motion setting, and every long page has a return-to-top link. Your browser’s Print command produces a simple reading copy.
