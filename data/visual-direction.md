# Visual direction and source basis

Revised 21 September 2026 after the author's review.

## What this site represents

Joyful Responsible Abundance asks what people could make possible with greatly expanded intelligence and productive capacity. Its imagery should express ambition, agency, difference and worthwhile lives. AI and robotics are part of how civilisations are made and maintained, rather than accessories added to an otherwise historical village.

The contribution connects personal intelligence, shared productive ownership, distributed civic capability, human self-alignment, culture and long-horizon civilisation. Each illustration has a job within that argument. Contemporary public life, sophisticated engineering, creative experiences and speculative horizons belong together.

## What the reading changes

The hero city connects surface life with robotic construction, material recovery and subterranean infrastructure. It is an imagined long horizon, rather than a depiction of an approved Minjerribah development.

The capability chapter shows serious civic compute, fabrication, heat recovery and contextual XR learning. The livelihoods chapter connects worker ownership with productive systems and freedom to create, learn and choose how to spend time.

Aura appears as a person-directed companion in creative, reflective and technically demanding situations. Wearable displays deliver relevant information within a task. Memories and personal goals remain part of the picture; Aura is broader than industrial assistance.

The health illustration shows co-design and research around an experimental Geode, not a completed medical service. The futures chapter reaches into generation-city life, planetary cultural connection and imaginative lunar cooperation. These horizons retain joy, music, nature, relationships and personal agency.

## Source differences

Some older project pages contain earlier launch, token, governance or treatment language. The current UNGA81 contribution and the author's explicit corrections govern this edition. Earlier webpages inform the history of the ideas; their claims are not silently promoted into current achievements. This visual review does not independently validate every factual claim in the source collection.

## Art direction

Use confident contemporary and futuristic design, rich colour, legible action and a sense of scale. Show a range of ages, backgrounds and ways to participate. Keep purposeful technology and human experience in the same frame. Avoid repetitive pastel courtyards, classical architecture, generic glass orbs and decorative horn tori.

Each image is a concept illustration. More speculative scenes and future research are named in their captions. The original document wording remains unchanged. Prompts, alternatives and source relationships are recorded in [hero metadata](image-prompts.json) and [chapter metadata](inline-illustrations.json).
